# Arranging the window

Every part of the window is a pane: New QSO, Worked before, Station, Map, Log, FTx monitor, Cluster, Awards, Band map, Watch list, Propagation, Rotator and Notes. Some start as tabs behind others; click a tab to bring it to the front. Put them wherever suits your screen, and QRZero remembers the arrangement on this computer.

## Resizing

Drag the thin bar between two panes. Double-click the bar to share the space evenly. You can also click a bar and use the arrow keys.

## Moving a pane

Drag a pane's tab and drop it on another pane. While you drag, a blue outline shows where it will go:

- Drop near an **edge** of a pane to dock beside it: left, right, above or below.
- Drop in the **middle** to add it as another tab in that spot.
- Drop on a **tab strip** to put it among those tabs, where you let go.

## Hiding and showing panes

The **×** at the top right of a pane hides it. Show it again from **Layout** in the top bar, which lists every pane with a tick for the ones shown.

## Popping a pane out to its own window

The arrow button next to the **×** opens the pane in its own window (or right-click the pane's tab and pick **Pop out**), so you can put the FTx monitor, the cluster or the map on a second screen. Every pane except New QSO and Log can pop out. Clicking a decode, a spot or a QSO in a popped-out pane works just as it does in the main window.

Close the window, or click **Dock back**, to put the pane back. QRZero remembers where each window was and reopens it there next time. When you close QRZero, the popped-out windows close with it.

In a web browser, allow pop-ups for QRZero's page, or the window can't open.

## Saved layouts

Save arrangements you switch between, such as **Laptop** and **Shack, 2 screens**, or one for an FT8 evening:

1. Arrange the panes.
2. Choose **Layout**, **Save layout as…** and give it a name.

Pick a saved layout from **Layout** to switch to it, popped-out windows included. **Reset to default** goes back to the starting arrangement. Layouts, like your column choices, filters and other window settings, are kept in your log database, so they survive restarts and updates and come back with a restored backup.

**Lock panes** stops tabs being dragged or hidden by accident. You can still resize panes while they are locked.

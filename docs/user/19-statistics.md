# Statistics

Choose **Statistics…** from the **☰** menu for a summary of the current log. It is counted when the window opens and again when you change a filter (a few moments on a very large log); nothing runs in the background.

## Filters

The row above the summary narrows what is counted:

- **Period**: all dates, one calendar year, or a **Custom range** (first and last day, UTC, both included).
- **Band**: one band, or all bands.
- **Mode**: CW, Phone or Digital, or all modes. Which modes belong to which group is set in **Settings > Modes**.

The choices are counted from your log, so only years and bands you have QSOs on are listed. The filters apply to both tabs.

## Summary

**Copy summary** puts the whole text on the clipboard, with a first line naming the filters, so you can paste it into an email, a forum post or a note. The summary has these parts:

- **Totals**: QSOs, different callsigns, first and last QSO, days on the air, your busiest day, your longest run of days in a row with a QSO, and your farthest contact. The farthest contact needs your own grid on the QSO (MY_GRIDSQUARE); the other station is placed by its grid, or by the middle of its entity when it has none, so treat it as a good estimate.
- **DXCC progress by year**: for each year the QSOs made, the entities worked for the first time that year (**New**) and the running total of entities (**Total**). This always counts from the start of the log, so a year filter does not turn old entities into new ones. With a band or mode filter it shows your progress on that band or mode. Years are whole years even when a custom range starts or ends part way through.
- **By band**, **By mode** and **By continent**: QSOs for each, and for bands and continents the entities worked. A QSO logged with the ASCII submode (ASCI) counts as RTTY.
- **Busiest UTC hours**: the five hours of the day with the most QSOs.
- **Confirmation rates**: for LoTW, paper QSL, QRZ Logbook, Club Log and eQSL, how many QSOs were sent, how many came back confirmed, and the percentage. A confirmed QSO counts as sent even if it was never marked sent. QRZ and Club Log confirmations are shown here but don't count towards the [Awards](11-awards).
- **Most worked**: the ten entities and the ten callsigns with the most QSOs.

An entity counts as confirmed here when any of its QSOs has a LoTW or paper confirmation. The entity of each QSO is its DXCC field, or the country file when the field is blank. LoTW's entity replaces the stored one when you download confirmations, as in the Awards pane.

## Map

The **Map** tab shows every place you have worked as a dot on a flat world map, bigger for more QSOs. Point at a dot to see its grid square (or entity) and QSO count. A QSO is drawn in its 4-character grid square; if it has no grid it is drawn in the middle of its entity, so a few dots may sit slightly off from the real station.

The statistics cover every QSO in the log, including those made with archived callsigns.
